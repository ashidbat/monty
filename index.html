Monty — good food, a little closer

Bakeries, cafés and shops around Ulaanbaatar sell the good food they still have at closing time, at a lower price. Monty shows you what is left near you, takes the payment, and gives you a QR pass to collect it at the counter.

An interactive design prototype, in English and Mongolian. The shops, the food and the payment are demo data: no money moves and no order reaches a real counter.
